Primary Care

Your medical home for everything. Our primary care physicians take the time to build a complete picture of your health — not just the reason for today's visit. Annual physicals, chronic disease management, sick visits, and preventive care under one familiar roof.

Schedule a Visit
What's Included
  • Annual physical exams and wellness screenings
  • Chronic disease management — diabetes, hypertension, asthma
  • Acute illness and injury evaluation
  • Immunizations and travel medicine
  • Prescription management and medication reviews
  • Coordination with specialists within Meridian
  • Secure patient portal with 24-hour messaging
Cardiology

Preventive and interventional cardiology delivered by fellowship-trained cardiologists. We offer comprehensive cardiac evaluations, advanced imaging, and ongoing heart health management — all without the 6-week specialist wait.

Schedule a Visit
What's Included
  • EKG and echocardiogram interpretation
  • Cardiac stress testing and Holter monitoring
  • Hypertension and cholesterol management
  • Atrial fibrillation evaluation and management
  • Heart failure monitoring programs
  • Pre-surgical cardiac clearance
  • Risk stratification and lifestyle counseling
Sports Medicine

Injury evaluation, rehabilitation planning, and performance optimization — for athletes from high school to professional level. Our sports medicine team works directly with Los Angeles sports organizations and brings that same standard to every patient.

Schedule a Visit
What's Included
  • Acute sports injury evaluation and treatment
  • Concussion management protocol
  • Platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy
  • Return-to-play clearances
  • Movement assessment and biomechanics review
  • Pre-participation sports physicals
  • Nutrition and performance optimization consultations
Internal Medicine

Expert management of complex adult conditions requiring more than a standard primary care visit. Our internal medicine physicians are trained to investigate, diagnose, and manage multi-system illnesses that other practices refer out.

Schedule a Visit
What's Included
  • Diabetes and metabolic syndrome management
  • Thyroid and endocrine disorder treatment
  • Autoimmune disease evaluation and co-management
  • Kidney and liver function monitoring
  • Complex medication management
  • Hospitalist coordination and discharge planning
  • Advanced lab panel interpretation
Women's Health

Comprehensive, whole-person women's healthcare from preventive screenings to hormonal health. Our physicians build long-term relationships — not just annual visits — with every patient in their care.
